These Subscription Terms explain how paid Plead subscriptions work. They form part of our [Terms & Conditions](/terms/).

**Which store?** Plead is available on iPhone and Android. Your purchase is processed by the store you downloaded Plead from: Apple’s App Store on iPhone, or Google Play (Google Play Billing) on Android. Where these terms refer to “the store”, they mean that store.

## 1. Plans

- **Weekly, monthly and annual plans** are offered on both iPhone and Android. The price of each plan is the localised price shown by the store at the time you subscribe (by Apple in the App Store, or by Google Play), and each renews automatically unless cancelled through that store’s subscription controls.
- Eligible users receive a 3-day free trial before the first annual charge. The monthly and weekly plans have no free trial.
- **Promotional annual pricing** may be offered to some users from time to time, on either store. Any promotional price, its duration and the standard price it renews at are shown by Apple or Google Play before you confirm the purchase.
- Plead is a paid app: an active subscription is needed to use it.
- Only one linked partner needs an active subscription for both linked accounts to get access to Plead while the couple remains linked, subject to the product entitlement rules.

## 2. Free trial

The 3-day trial applies only to the annual plan, on both iPhone and Android, and only where the store determines the customer is eligible: Apple on the App Store, or Google Play on Android. If the trial is not cancelled before it ends, the store will charge the annual subscription price shown at purchase. Eligibility and billing are controlled by the App Store or Google Play.

## 3. Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through the store’s subscription settings within the period the store requires. Deleting the Plead app or account does not automatically cancel a subscription with Apple or Google Play. To cancel or change a plan:

- **iPhone (App Store):** use Apple’s [Manage Subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) page, or on iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
- **Android (Google Play):** use Google Play’s [Subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) page, or on your phone open the Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

## 4. Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android), not by us. Refund requests should generally be made through the store you paid, subject to applicable law and Apple’s processes or Google Play’s policies.

## 5. Linked-partner entitlement

Access to Plead follows the current couple entitlement rules: the couple's subscription covers both linked partners. If partners unlink, the paying user retains the benefit associated with their qualifying active purchase; the non-paying former partner may lose access to Plead unless separately entitled. The same applies if the paying partner deletes their account.

## 6. Restoring purchases

If you reinstall Plead or change device, open Plead and use Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the store account that made the purchase: the Apple ID on iPhone, or the Google account on Android.
