## 3. What Plead is

Plead is an entertainment and relationship app for everyday disagreements. It lets partners present cases, evidence and responses to AI systems that generate juror findings and a judge-style ruling. Plead is not a court, legal service, therapist, mediator, emergency service or provider of legal, medical, financial or other professional advice.

- Either person may unlink. When partners unlink, cases still in progress end as a mistrial and cases that already have a verdict are closed; both people keep their historical case records, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
- Do not impersonate another person or create an account without authority.

AI outputs can be incomplete, inaccurate, inconsistent or inappropriate. Verdicts are generated for entertainment and relationship discussion. Do not rely on Plead to make decisions involving safety, abuse, legal rights, health, money, employment, housing or other high-stakes matters. The app may refuse or stop cases that appear to involve serious safety concerns.

## 7. Winner-selected judgements

After a verdict, Plead may offer the winner case-relevant judgement options. These are suggestions for consensual relationship activities only. They are not legally enforceable, and no user is required to perform an unsafe, illegal, coercive, humiliating or unwanted activity. If either person is uncomfortable, they should not proceed.
